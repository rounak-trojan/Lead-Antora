FAQ

Questions institute owners ask

Do I need a website?

No. Every center gets a hosted enquiry page. Put its link in your Instagram bio, YouTube descriptions and Facebook page button.

Can I run several branches?

Yes. Each center has its own team, courses, sources, integrations and data. Centers never see each other's leads.

Can counsellors see each other's leads?

No. Counsellors see only the leads assigned to them. Admins see the whole center.

Can I bring my existing leads?

Yes. Import a CSV with name and phone at minimum. Duplicates by phone number are skipped automatically.

What happens when the same student enquires twice?

The enquiry is added to the existing lead's timeline and the follow-up moves to today. A lead marked lost is reopened automatically.
